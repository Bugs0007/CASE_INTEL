02 · Read the order

The order arrives
as a scan. You get
what you must do.

Each order PDF is summarised once, when it arrives: what the court did, then your side's directions, kept apart from the other side's. Your directions become tasks with due dates.

  • Due dates come from a fixed table of phrases like “within four weeks”, not from the AI.
  • Unreadable, very short or routine adjournment orders skip the AI entirely.
  • Only your side's directions become tasks, and only once your party role is known.

06 · Whose file is it

Every matter has
an owner. Every
query asks who's
asking.

each square, one record · lit: yours · dark: someone else's, never returned
Sign-in

One account, one advocate.

Sign up with Google, or from an invitation link with a username and password. Google sends Case Intel your name and verified email address, never your Google password.

Per-advocate isolation

Every record has an owner.

Cases, documents, hearings, fees: each row carries its owner, and every list, view and edit is filtered to the advocate signed in. A test fails if a new endpoint is added without being scoped or deliberately reviewed.

Owner-scoped retrieval

Case Bot only searches your documents.

The search takes the owner as a required input and filters every leg by it, meaning and keyword alike. Narrowing to one case narrows within your documents; it never replaces the owner filter.

What it won't do

  • It won't read cause lists for seven High Courts (Allahabad, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab & Haryana) or the Madurai bench of Madras. The eCourts portal serves no usable list for them, so those matters say “Not covered” rather than “Not listed.”
  • It doesn't track Supreme Court cases.
  • It won't tell two advocates with the same name apart. It shows the court's own record and lets you judge.
  • It won't hard-block a conflict. It warns; you decide.
  • It won't apply limitation exceptions for you. Sections 12(2), 4 and 5 stay as notes.
  • It won't send routine adjournment orders to the AI at all.
  • It won't file anything on your behalf.
  • It isn't affiliated with eCourts or with any court.
